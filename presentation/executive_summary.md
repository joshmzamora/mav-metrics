# Executive Summary

## Problem

Player marketability is usually discussed with simple signals like fame or follower count. That misses players who have strong performance, rising attention, or sponsor potential before the market notices.

## Solution

Mav Metrics is a simple public-data framework that scores players across six pillars:

1. performance
2. reach
3. attention
4. engagement
5. momentum
6. brand fit

## Key Output

The most useful output is the **Marketability Gap**:

```text
Marketability Gap = Actual Marketability - Expected Marketability
```

A player with strong performance but lower marketability than expected may be commercially underdeveloped.

## Recommendations

- Use the gap to find under-marketed players.
- Use momentum to act before players become expensive.
- Use player cards to turn scores into specific content and sponsorship actions.
- Validate the model against jersey sales, digital views, or other real fan behavior.

## Final Point

The model is intentionally simple, explainable, and reusable. It gives a team, player, agent, or brand a better starting point for deciding where to invest attention.
