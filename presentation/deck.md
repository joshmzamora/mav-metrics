# Mav Metrics Presentation Deck

## Slide 1 — Title
**Mav Metrics: Finding the NBA's Under-Marketed Players**

One-line story: Basketball performance explains part of marketability, but the most valuable opportunities are in the gap.

## Slide 2 — The Problem
Teams, agents, and sponsors need a better way to answer:

> Which players have commercial value now, and which players are about to become more valuable?

## Slide 3 — Why Followers Are Not Enough
Follower count misses engagement quality, performance credibility, attention momentum, and sponsor fit.

## Slide 4 — Our Framework
Six pillars:

1. Performance
2. Reach
3. Attention
4. Engagement
5. Momentum
6. Brand fit

## Slide 5 — Model Formula
```text
Marketability Score =
  25% Performance
+ 20% Reach
+ 20% Attention
+ 15% Engagement
+ 10% Momentum
+ 10% Brand
```

All variables are converted to percentiles. Large counts are log-scaled first.

## Slide 6 — The Key Insight: Marketability Gap
```text
Marketability Gap = Actual Marketability - Expected Marketability
```

A player with a negative gap may be commercially underdeveloped relative to performance.

## Slide 7 — Marketability Map
Show the scatterplot:

- X-axis: Performance Score
- Y-axis: Marketability Score
- Bubble size: Momentum Score

Quadrants:

- High performance / high marketability: proven stars
- High performance / low marketability: hidden opportunity
- Low performance / high marketability: brand outperformers
- Low performance / low marketability: low priority
