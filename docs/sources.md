# Sources to Use

Use this as the citation checklist for the final deck.

## Basketball performance

- NBA Stats: https://www.nba.com/stats
- Basketball Reference: https://www.basketball-reference.com

## Attention and public interest

- Google Trends: https://trends.google.com
- Wikipedia Pageviews: https://pageviews.wmcloud.org
- GDELT Project: https://www.gdeltproject.org
- Google News search/manual article count

## Social and fan engagement

- Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube public profile counts
- Manual recent-post engagement sample

## Validation

- NBA official jersey sales releases
- NBA official social/digital view releases
- All-Star voting results

## Methodology references

- Nielsen athlete influencer/marketability research
- SportsPro / NorthStar athlete marketability framework
- Academic athlete brand image research

For each source used in the final deck, record the date accessed and the exact time window measured.
